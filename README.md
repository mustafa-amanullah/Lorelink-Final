# 🧵 Lorelink

A social storytelling app where you share narratives, connect with fellow storytellers, and explore compelling stories from around the world.

## ✨ Features

- 📖 **Create Stories** - Share your narratives with rich text and multimedia
- ❤️ **Like Stories** - Show appreciation for stories you love
- 💬 **Comment** - Engage in conversations about stories you enjoy
- 🔄 **Share Stories** - Spread captivating narratives with your network
- 🔖 **Save Stories** - Bookmark stories for later reading
- 👥 **Follow Storytellers** - Build your community and customize your feed
- 🔍 **Explore** - Discover new stories and authors
- 👤 **Profiles** - View author bios, their stories, and contributions
- 🌙 **Dark Mode** - Comfortable reading experience anytime
- ⚙️ **Settings** - Personalize your Lorelink experience

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Sign Up
1. Open the Lorelink app
2. Tap **Sign up**
3. Enter your name, username, and password
4. Complete your profile setup
5. Ready to share your first story!

### Sign In
1. Tap **Sign in**
2. Enter your username and password
3. Access your feed and start discovering stories

## 🎯 Quick Tips

- Follow storytellers to see their latest stories in your feed
- Click on usernames to visit author profiles
- Like and comment to engage with the story community
- Save stories you want to revisit later
- Use explore to discover stories across different genres and themes
- Share stories that inspire you with your followers
- Check dark mode in settings for late-night reading

## ❓ Questions?

- **Can I delete my stories?** - Yes, delete your own stories anytime from your profile
- **Can I edit stories?** - Currently you can delete and repost; contact support for edit requests
- **Who can see my stories?** - Everyone! Stories are public and discoverable
- **How do I change my username?** - Contact support for username changes
- **Is my data safe?** - Yes, all passwords are encrypted and your data is secure
- **Can I see who liked my story?** - Yes, view the likes list on any story
- **How do I report inappropriate content?** - Use the report feature on any story or comment

## 🏗️ Tech Stack

### Backend
- Node.js & Express
- Firebase Authentication & Database
- AI-powered content recommendations

### Frontend
- React Native with Expo
- React Navigation for routing
- React Native Paper for UI components

## 📱 Platform Support

- iOS
- Android

## 🤝 Community Guidelines

- Be respectful and kind to fellow storytellers
- Share original content or properly attribute sources
- No hate speech, harassment, or explicit content
- Respect others' privacy and intellectual property
- Report violations to help keep Lorelink safe

## 🌟 About Lorelink

Lorelink is built for storytellers, by storytellers. Whether you're sharing personal experiences, fictional adventures, or insightful narratives, Lorelink connects you with an audience that appreciates authentic stories. Join our growing community today!
